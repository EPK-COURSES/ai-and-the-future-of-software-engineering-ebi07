Reflection

This research changed my understanding of AI in software engineering. The most surprising finding was that AI adoption continues to increase even though developer trust in AI accuracy is declining. This shows that developers find AI useful for improving productivity, but they still recognize its limitations.

My biggest concern is the potential for security vulnerabilities and incorrect code generated by AI tools. If developers rely too heavily on AI without reviewing its outputs, software quality and security could be affected. However, I also see many opportunities. AI can automate repetitive tasks, speed up development, assist with debugging, and help developers learn new technologies more efficiently.

I believe skills such as critical thinking, problem-solving, software design, and security awareness will remain important because AI cannot fully replace human judgment. Software engineers will need to adapt by learning how to use AI effectively while continuing to develop strong technical foundations.

Before conducting this research, I viewed AI mainly as a coding assistant. Now I see it as a technology that is transforming the entire software development process. Personally, I plan to use AI as a productivity tool while continuing to strengthen my programming, design, and analytical skills to remain effective in the future.
